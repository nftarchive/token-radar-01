Left off the board, on purpose

These are the biggest pre-token names in the snapshot. None of them is a launch candidate: exchanges will never issue a protocol token, and bridges and staked products are a token that already trades, moved somewhere else. Large names the house left off its watchlist are left off here too: the snapshot cannot show whether they are a sub-product of another protocol. Ranking them would be ranking the wrong thing.

    How the ranking works

    implied value = TVL × sector multiple × growth × maturity × breadth × size

    The sector multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL of the tokenised protocols in the same category, taken from this very snapshot. Growth counts at half its face value and is capped. Maturity caps at two years of track record. Breadth caps at six chains. Size is sublinear: doubling TVL lifts implied value by less than double. Market cap here is circulating cap, not fully diluted value, so the read is conservative.
